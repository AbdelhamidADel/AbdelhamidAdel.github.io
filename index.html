Root Cause

Full refresh daily — truncate, reload 1.7PB including all history. Massive I/O, timeouts, dashboard blackouts lasting days.

Solution

  • Incremental loading — new & modified records only
  • Optimized merge/upsert strategies
  • Day-1 adjustment job for backdated transactions
  • Quarterly reconciliation without full reloads
